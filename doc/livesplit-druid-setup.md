LiveSplit One Druid can be ran on all platforms. It is in its infancy compared to traditional LiveSplit, but it gets the job done regardless with support for auto-splitting.

## Instructions

First, install [LiveSplit One Druid](https://github.com/AlexKnauth/livesplit-one-druid?tab=readme-ov-file#installation-instructions). Once it's running, right click on it and select `Open Auto-splitter...`. Select the `spyro-rt-autosplitter.wasm` file.

That's it for configuring the auto-splitter in general, though you may want to open splits and configure a layout to your liking (you may wish to configure your layout on the [web version](https://one.livesplit.org/) as it is more stable in this regard). As for configuring the auto-splitter's settings, you can follow the guide [here](settings.md).
